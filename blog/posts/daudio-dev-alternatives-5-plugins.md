## ToneLab

![https://vector-dsp.com](https://csuxjmfbwmkxiegfpljm.supabase.co/storage/v1/object/public/blog-images/organization-30746/1780711145298_vector-dsp.jpg)

### At a Glance

Five independent effect lanes let you apply different effects to distinct frequency ranges inside a single plugin insert. That single-insert approach replaces complex parallel routing and keeps the DAW session tidy while you sculpt sound.

ToneLab ships in VST3, AU, and AAX formats and targets mix engineers, producers, and sound designers who need frequency-aware processing.

### Core Features

- **Five independent effect lanes** each include a dedicated EQ, an effect slot, and a mix control for layered processing.
- Per-lane EQ lets you target low, mid, or high bands so effects only touch the frequencies you want.
- Single-insert design reduces the need for multi-send routing chains and saves DAW tracks and busses.
- Built on an industry standard architecture with support for **VST3, AU, AAX** and real-time, low latency performance.
- Effects include Chorus, Distortion, Reverb, Delay, and Saturation with intuitive lane routing.

Vector DSP builds ToneLab from a background in professional-grade audio software and advanced digital signal processing. The company emphasizes thoughtful DSP design, industry standard frameworks, and real-time performance constraints for production work.

### Key Differentiator

ToneLab’s defining capability is letting you apply effects precisely to specific frequency ranges using independent EQ lanes inside one insert. That design moves frequency-aware processing from elaborate routing sessions into a single, controllable instance.

### Pros

- Applying reverb, distortion, or delay to narrow frequency bands reduces bleed and muddiness. The result is clearer mixes with less masking between instruments.

- Consolidates parallel chains. Instead of multiple aux sends and busses, you can collapse stacked sends into one plugin instance and reclaim tracks and CPU headroom.

- Compatible with major host formats and built with attention to DSP accuracy, so it integrates into Pro Tools, Logic, and modern DAWs without format compromises.

- Interface focuses on layered sound design. Per-lane mix and EQ knobs let you audition changes quickly and lock in complex textures without deep menu diving.

- A demo mode lets you try the plugin before buying, and purchases include free updates within the v1.x line so minor revisions do not require additional payment.

### Cons

- Effect choices are fixed to the five lane types, which limits users who want custom or modular effect routing beyond Chorus, Distortion, Reverb, Delay, and Saturation.

### Notable Integrations

- VST3
- AU (Audio Units)
- AAX (Avid Audio eXtention, Pro Tools)

These formats cover the common professional DAW hosts used by mix engineers and producers.

### Who It's For

Producers, mix engineers, and sound designers who want frequency-aware effects without rebuilding parallel routing. If you work in sessions that need precise band-limited processing and you use hosts that support VST3, AU, or AAX, ToneLab fits into that workflow.

### Unique Value Proposition

Replacing a multi-send parallel chain with a single plugin insert changes session architecture. You keep the same tonal control but remove multiple aux tracks, save CPU, and shorten project setup time. For an engineer juggling quick revisions across dozens of tracks, that arithmetic adds up in saved time and session stability.

### Real World Use Case

A producer loads ToneLab on a vocal bus, sets Lane 2 to midband reverb, and keeps sibilance and low end dry. The vocal gains presence without the highs washing the mix. The process takes one insert and two quick EQ moves rather than a three-bus routing scheme.

### Pricing

One-time purchase at $73.99. The license includes all future v1.x updates and a demo mode for evaluation prior to purchase.

**Website:** https://vector-dsp.com
