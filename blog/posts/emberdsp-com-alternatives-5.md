That algorithmic detection lets you address resonances and transient spikes in real time. For busy mixing engineers, that means less manual EQ surgery and faster passes to a polished balance.

### Pros

The plugins deliver transparent, high quality processing that keeps the original character of voices and instruments while removing trouble spots. Platform support includes Apple Silicon and a broad set of DAWs, which keeps the tools usable across desktop systems. The company cites active user feedback as a development driver, and that shows in controls that quickly move from subtle correction to creative shaping.

Engineers who prefer hands off correction plus manual fine tuning will appreciate the control layout.

### Real World Use Case

A mixing engineer uses **Soothe3** to control vocal resonances while tracking, removing build up that would normally require repeated EQ passes.
