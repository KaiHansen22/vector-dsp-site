Low latency operation lets you insert a plugin during tracking, while adjustable parameters like detail, response, tilt, and max cut let you fine tune results. The suite also includes linear phase options and multichannel routing up to 9.1.6 channels.

### Key Differentiator

The standout capability is the combination of **automatic, real-time detection** with multichannel support and deep parameter control. That lets you find and remove resonances quickly without manual band hunting. This approach suits mixes that move between stereo and immersive deliverables.

### Pros

The processing delivers a transparent tonal result that reviewers describe as natural, making it easy to reduce harsh frequencies without audible coloration. Plugins like **soothe3** and **spiff** provide detailed controls for both surgical resonance reduction and subtle tone shaping, and linear phase modes help preserve transient character. Low latency and zero latency tracking modes let you use these tools while recording or during final mix passes.

### Cons

- The interface exposes many parameters, which some engineers find complex and slow to learn.

- Price per plugin can feel high compared to simpler single purpose tools.

- Compatibility is limited to recent Windows and macOS builds, and some products lack native ARM Apple Silicon support.

If you prefer minimal plugins with one knob control, the deep parameter set here will slow you down. Projects constrained to older operating systems or Apple Silicon only workflows will run into compatibility limits. ### Who It's For

Mixing and mastering engineers, sound designers, and producers who need precise resonance control and immersive channel routing will find this suite valuable. An engineer loads **soothe3** on a vocal bus and lets the automatic detector remove narrow harsh resonances during mix prep. The plugin trims sibilance and ringing so the engineer avoids hours of manual notch EQ. The result is a polished vocal that sits cleanly without losing presence.
