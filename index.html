Technology

How We Build

Three things ToneLab lets you stop doing by hand — and the engineering underneath each one.

01
Keep your low end tight while the reverb sits in the mids

Each lane's EQ decides where its effect lands, so a long reverb never washes into your 808. Real-time performance is the constraint every processing decision is made against — latency and CPU efficiency first, not as an afterthought.

02
Replace a five-bus parallel chain with one insert

Five sends, five buses, five EQ instances — rebuilt every session. ToneLab makes that routing permanent. Every parameter, algorithm and interface decision is deliberate; nothing is added for the sake of it.

03
Sculpt band-specific textures in a single pass

Saturate the low mids, chorus the air, leave the vocal range untouched — without leaving the channel. Built in C++ on the same architecture professional audio companies use, and shipped in the formats you actually run: AU, VST3 and AAX.
