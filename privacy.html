Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026  ·  Vector DSP

01 — Overview

Vector DSP is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect, why we collect it, and how it is handled. We collect the minimum data necessary to operate our licensing system and improve our products.

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes.

The machine ID is a one-way hash derived from your MAC address, username, and computer name. It cannot be reversed to identify you personally and is used solely for license binding.

03 — How We Use Your Data

  • To process and fulfil your software purchase
  • To deliver your license key and activation instructions
  • To validate your license when the plugin checks in every 7 days
  • To respond to support requests you initiate
  • To send important product updates (you can opt out at any time)

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose beyond operating our licensing system.

04 — Third-Party Services

Lemon Squeezy

We use Lemon Squeezy as our payment and licensing platform. When you purchase ToneLab, Lemon Squeezy processes your payment and stores your purchase record. Their privacy policy is available at lemonsqueezy.com/privacy.

PACE Anti-Piracy

The AAX version of ToneLab uses PACE Anti-Piracy signing technology required by Avid for Pro Tools compatibility. PACE does not receive or store any of your personal data through this integration.

05 — Data Retention

License cache data stored locally on your device remains until you uninstall the software or manually delete it. Purchase records held by Lemon Squeezy are retained in accordance with their data retention policy and applicable financial regulations.

06 — Your Rights

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Opt out of marketing communications

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at privacy@vector-dsp.com.

07 — Cookies

Our website uses minimal cookies necessary for site functionality. We do not use tracking cookies or third-party advertising cookies.

08 — Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be posted on this page with an updated date. Continued use of our software after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
