Refund Policy

Last updated: April 2026  ·  Vector DSP

Our Commitment

We want you to be confident in your purchase. If ToneLab doesn't work as expected on your system, we will make it right.

ToneLab is available as a fully functional demo before purchase. We encourage you to try the demo in your DAW to confirm compatibility before buying. Because of this, and because digital software cannot be returned once delivered, our refund policy is structured as follows.

01 — Before Activation

If you have purchased ToneLab but have not yet activated your license key, you may request a full refund within 14 days of your purchase date.

To request a refund, email support@vector-dsp.com with your order number and we will process it promptly.

02 — After Activation

Once a license key has been activated, refunds are generally not available as the software has been delivered and used. However, we will consider requests in the following circumstances:

  • The software has a critical bug that prevents it from functioning in your DAW and we are unable to resolve it within a reasonable timeframe
  • A duplicate purchase was made in error

Contact us at support@vector-dsp.com with your order number and a description of the issue. We review all requests individually.

03 — Technical Issues

Before requesting a refund for technical reasons, please contact our support team — most compatibility issues can be resolved quickly. We support:

  • macOS 11.0 and later (Apple Silicon and Intel)
  • Logic Pro, Ableton Live, Cubase, Studio One, Reaper (VST3 / AU)
  • Pro Tools 2020 and later (AAX)

04 — How to Request

Email support@vector-dsp.com with:

  • Your order number (from your Lemon Squeezy receipt)
  • The email address used at purchase
  • The reason for your request

We aim to respond within 2 business days. Approved refunds are processed via your original payment method.

Contact

Refund requests and purchase questions: support@vector-dsp.com
