How it works

Capture once. Render anything.

No screen capture, no re-analysing a bounce. The analysis is taken from inside the session, sample-accurate to the playhead, so every render lines up with the music to the frame.

01 — CAPTURE

Drop the plugin on the master

Add children to the stems you care about — kick, 808, hats, vocal. Play the song through once. Specular writes a capture file with level, bands, transients, pitch and the tempo map for every source.

02 — ROUTE

Decide what drives what

Open the capture in Specular Studio. Pick a look, then wire stems to it: the kick moves the geometry, the 808 sets the hue, the hats light the detail. Presets get you there in one click; the cross-wire panel goes as deep as you want.

03 — RENDER

Export on your own GPU

Offline, so there is no realtime budget: full supersampling, linear light, 10-bit HEVC if you want it. Portrait or landscape, mp4 with the audio muxed in, ready to post.

What ships

Two pieces, one license

Perpetual license, the same way ToneLab works. Nothing phones home, nothing expires, nothing is metered.

At launch Specular + Specular Studio

  • PluginSpecular — the capture plugin. VST3, AU and AAX. Parent on the master, children on stems. Diagnostic meters, not a preview, so it costs the session nothing.
  • RendererSpecular Studio — the offline renderer. Ten looks, six palettes, per-stem routing, GPU render to H.264 or 10-bit HEVC.
  • PlatformmacOS first, Apple Silicon native.
  • LicensingOne-time purchase, not a subscription. Activates locally.

Later Tiers on the same app

  • ProExport the motion, not just the video: keyframes for After Effects, Blender, TouchDesigner and MIDI.
  • LiveThe same looks driving lights, haze and video on stage over DMX, OSC and MIDI.
Both are license tiers inside Specular Studio, not separate apps. No dates yet; the waitlist hears first.
